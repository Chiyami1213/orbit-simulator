Orbit Simulator is installed.

GETTING STARTED
  1. Open the left menu and choose Modules, then pick a module.
  2. Load a homework or exam problem, or enter your own values.
  3. Enter your name under Settings > Figure Title (printed in every figure title).

KEYS
  F5   refresh the screen          F2   screenshot
  F11  full screen                 S    settings          M    fold the menu

YOUR FILES
  Settings, screenshots, exports and logs are saved to
  %APPDATA%\Orbit Simulator\   and are never uploaded.
  Uninstalling keeps this folder, so your settings survive a reinstall.

HELP AND SOURCE CODE

  "Orbit Simulator Help"). The project page, installation notes and issue
